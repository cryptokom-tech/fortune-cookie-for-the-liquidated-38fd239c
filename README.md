One funny fortune-cookie line for a crypto trader who has just
been liquidated, written by a small Python program for the
[Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader](https://make.fun/c/u-clever-harbor-75-fortune-cookie-for-the-liquidated-38fd239c)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The line

> The stars say your portfolio will rise again — just not the one you liquidated at 3 a.m.

A fortune cookie always promises a bright future, so the slip
opens exactly like a real one — "The stars say your portfolio
will rise again" — then the swerve: the portfolio that rises is
not the one you just liquidated. The 3 a.m. is the hour the
trader is reading it at, wallet empty, humour stubborn. Rueful
and warm, never cruel: the joke lands on the loss, not on the
trader.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (18 words,
88 characters, UTF-8). No network, no dependencies, no secrets
— just the line.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
